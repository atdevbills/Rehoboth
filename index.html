WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Rehoboth

Rehoboth Baptist Church, known as the House of Testimonies, is a welcoming community in Kweiman, Accra. We gather to worship God, learn from His Word, and grow together in faith and fellowship.

Whether you are new to the area or looking for a church family, we invite you to join us at any of our weekly meetings.

MEET OUR PASTOR

rev. emmanuel asiedu-appiah

Rev. Emmanuel Asiedu-Appiah is a prominent Ghanaian minister and the Head Pastor of the Rehoboth Baptist Church located in Kweiman, Accra. He is widely recognized for his radio ministry, relationship counseling, and strong digital presence through podcasted sermons.
